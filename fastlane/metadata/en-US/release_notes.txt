Code in colour, diagrams that no longer hold the app up, and safer sync.

• Code blocks are coloured by language — Swift, Python, JavaScript, Go, Rust, SQL and 30 more — in the editor, in reading mode and in exports.
• Opening a note with Mermaid diagrams stalls the app far less, and diagrams keep their own proportions; one drawn once appears without redrawing next time.
• Export as Image: the note as one picture, ready to share. Very long notes are cut; Export as PDF has everything.
• Reading mode spaces Chinese and Japanese from Latin letters and digits, like the editor.
• Share files from any app — Files, Mail, Photos and more — straight into your knowledge base.
• Notes with pictures open without freezing; long notes open faster.
• Safer sync across your devices: a note deleted on one and edited on another comes back under its own name; AGENTS.md, README.md and SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md no longer leave conflict copies; Inkstone's own folders reach your other devices even while empty; an emptied repository no longer stops sync with "branch not found".
• The assistant now remembers your API key and model when you reopen Inkstone. If it asks once more, enter it again; after that it stays.
• Settings › Knowledge Base opens at the top.
