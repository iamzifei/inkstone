What's new in 0.4

• Templates: keep them in a folder (Templates by default) and insert one into the open note, with {{title}}, {{date}} and {{time}} filled in.
• Export a note as a PDF (A4 pages) or a self-contained HTML file, from the More menu.
• Command palette: every command, found by typing a few letters. In the More menu, or ⇧⌘P on a keyboard.
• Notes in system search: titles and text are added to this device's search index, and a result opens the note. Can be turned off in Settings.
• Shortcuts actions: New Note and Add to Today's Note, for Siri, the Action button and home-screen shortcuts.
• iPhone Duo: the caret and scroll position stay put when you fold or unfold.
• Background sync carries on where it stopped when iOS ends a long first sync early.
