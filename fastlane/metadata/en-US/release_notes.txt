Safer sync across your devices.

• A note deleted on one device and edited on another comes back under its own name.
• AGENTS.md, README.md and SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md no longer leave conflict copies when two devices refresh them.
• Inkstone's own knowledge-base folders now reach your other devices even while empty, so its entry files stop being rewritten back and forth.
• A repository emptied of every file no longer stops sync with "branch not found".
