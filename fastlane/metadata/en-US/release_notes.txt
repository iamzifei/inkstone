What's new in 0.3

• Find and replace inside a note, from the toolbar or ⌘F on a keyboard.
• iPhone Duo: half-open, the note sits on one side of the crease and its backlinks, outline and properties on the other.
• iPhone: larger rows, swipe to bookmark, rename or move to trash, a bottom bar to jump to or create a note, and the inspector as a sheet.
• GitHub sync is safer. Fixed cases that could delete or bring back notes: renaming a note by changing only its capitalisation, hidden files in the repository, files that could not be read or were still in iCloud, and very large repositories. Typing during a sync is never overwritten.
• Sync health in Settings: last successful sync, failed files, conflict copies, and a preview of the next sync.
• Markdown reads the way Obsidian reads it: prices such as A$220 are no longer taken for maths, bare web addresses are links, and tags follow Obsidian's rules.
• Large vaults open without a pause, and long notes stay quick to type in.
• Rebuild Index in Settings, for when files were changed elsewhere with their dates preserved.
