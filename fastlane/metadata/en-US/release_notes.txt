Inkstone is now an AI knowledge base.

• Create one in three steps: a name, a place (iCloud Drive, a folder, or a folder you already have) — the folders, rules and entry files are made for you.
• Drop anything in — notes, PDFs, photos, recordings. The first time you choose copy or move; copy is the default.
• It is filed for you: by your own rules, then by the on-device model, named by date and topic, duplicates put aside. Anything unsure waits for you. Nothing is ever deleted, and every round can be undone.
• AGENTS.md, README.md and SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md are written and kept up to date, so any AI assistant knows what is where.
• Edit the organizing rules in plain words in Settings › Knowledge Base.
• Share links and text to Inkstone from other apps; in a knowledge base they are filed too.
