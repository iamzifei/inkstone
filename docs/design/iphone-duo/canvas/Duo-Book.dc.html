Reading notes — Calvino

Six memos: Lightness, quickness, exactitude, visibility, multiplicity. The fifth was never written.

Lightness

Perseus looks at Medusa only in the bronze shield — indirect vision as a method.

Quickness

The economy of a story that skips what the reader can supply.

Exactitude

A well-defined plan, sharp images, language as precise as possible.
